# ABSTRACT

This strategic document formalizes a paradigm shift in the understanding and management of the Chinese economy. Inspired by the Theory of Rent Extraction (ToRE) [@rebour2000] and formalized through the Clifford algebra $\mathrm{Cl}(6,6)$, it proposes a discrete topological model that treats economics and geography as two facets of a single structure. It establishes six structural facts concerning the current crisis.

**1. The crisis is not an anomaly, but a conservation law.** The collapse of the real-estate market and the local government financing vehicle (LGFV) debt crisis are not governance failures, but the inevitable attainment of the "nodal phase" — the physical exhaustion of the stock of land value available for extraction.

**2. The immaterial is a morphological dead end.** The transition to artificial intelligence and carbon (the "Third Mutation") does not create the mass jobs that are needed. With an employment elasticity of 0.14 (versus 1.8 for construction), the immaterial engenders an "algorithmic stasis".

**3. The double geo-economic Stasis.** Post-2018 China projects simultaneously onto economic Stasis ($S_{\text{eco}}$) and territorial Stasis ($S_{\text{geo}}$). The pair $(S_{\text{eco}}, S_{\text{geo}})$ is classified as **Ephemeral** with a viability index $\mathcal{S} = 0.044$: structurally coherent but dynamically ready to collapse.

**4. The rarity of viability.** Out of the **400 situations** that the system can occupy, **only 11 are viable** (2.75%). Sustainability is the topological exception, not the rule. The TRR makes it possible to move from the Stasis pair ($\mathcal{S} = 0.044$) to a viable pair ($\mathcal{S} \approx 0.226$), i.e. a **viability factor of ~5**.

**5. The destruction of wealth can be avoided.** For the first time in two millennia, a Chinese state has at its disposal a peaceful tool — the Territorial Regeneration Rent (TRR) — to short-circuit the fatal cycle of dynastic collapses.

**6. The TRR as the foundation of a new diplomacy — the diplomacy of regeneration.** Beyond China, the TRR founds a new diplomacy. By demonstrating that it is possible to exit the "nodal phase" without massive destruction of wealth, China offers the Global South a structural alternative to Western imperialism. **Rather than exporting an extraction model (characterized by logistical colonialization and factories vulnerable to host-country sovereign rent), China can export a topological regeneration model: the Two Mountains Bank, the integration of GEP into KPIs, and Regenerative Usufruct Certificates.** This "Two Mountains diplomacy" positions the Economic Celestial Way (*Tiandao Jingji*, 天道经济) as a new viable civilizational standard.

## The TRR as the most robust trajectory

From a topological point of view, exiting Stasis requires the controlled crossing of threshold $N_4$. The TRR is the operator $T_{\text{mixed}}$ that activates this passage. It combines three structural components (Wood, Earth, Metal) as well as Fire (shocks/innovation) and Water (money/credit), synthesized into 3 reforms:

* **Two Mountains Bank ($T_{\text{water}}$):** securitization of the GEP to bypass LGFV debt.
* **Integration of the GEP into key performance indicators ($T_{\text{structure}}$):** reform of political incentives.
* **Regenerative Usufruct Certificate ($T_{\text{fire}}$):** creation of a new value-distribution cycle.

The TRR does not impose a single trajectory. It offers a space of possibilities structured by topological constraints: three variants share the same structure (activation of thresholds $P_4$ and $N_4$, respect for nilpotency, controlled passage), and differ in their intermediate situations, hence in the speed and robustness of the transition. The "pure" variant (horizon 2032) is the most direct; the "tempered growth" variant (horizon 2035) is the most realistic in the short term; the "rentier economy" variant (horizon 2040) is the most prudent but the slowest. The choice between these variants depends on political priorities (speed vs. stability) and on social constraints (capacity to absorb reforms).

## The spectral dashboard

This model offers the Party-State a governance tool without precedent, modelled on the four examinations of traditional Chinese medicine (望闻问切):

* **8 vital signs** (4 eco + 4 geo): Asymmetry ($\eta$, the Qi), dimension ($d$, harmony), gap (fragility), threshold ($R_{\text{threshold}}$, tension) — computed separately for each plane.
* **12 economic meridians** + **12 geographic meridians**: the 24 sectoral spectral leaves.
* **144 acupuncture points**: the 144 geo-economic states, allowing precise territorial targeting to unlock blockage nodes.

## What the model does not say

The report is a decision-support instrument, not an automatic forecasting device. Three limits must be kept in mind:

- **The epistemological status of the model.** The mathematical framework is established; the application to the Chinese economy is proposed, with partial empirical validation. The results (the 400 pairs, the 11 viable ones, the spectral thresholds) are model results, not observed facts. The body of the text uses "the model indicates" rather than "topology imposes".
- **Resistances and costs.** The TRR has losers (provincial bureaucracy, sectors backed by land rent, declining territories) and costs (jobs destroyed before being recreated, fiscal costs, political costs). Chapter 16 analyses them explicitly; they are not concealed.
- **The origin of certain constants.** The spectral thresholds ($S_3$ to $S_7$) are calibrated on the nine Chinese dynastic declines. Their research programme remains open. Operational steering uses values rounded to two decimal places, which are more robust than the five-decimal values of the technical document.

## Recommendation 1 — Activate the emergency protocol (immediate, 2026-2027)

**Observation:** the composite Stasis index is at 0.64, at the limit of the red alert (0.65). The four spectral observables are in the red zone. The classic stimulus tools (interest rates, quantitative easing) are saturated.

**Decision:** trigger the red-zone intervention protocol (Chapter 13.6):
- Freeze schemes based solely on land valuation.
- Activate the transition airlock, not volume-based stimulus.
- Quarterly monitoring of combined viability, not of cohesion alone.

**Owner:** State Council, via the NDRC.
**Indicator:** $\mathcal{I}_{\text{Stasis}}$ stable or declining at 12 months.

## Recommendation 2 — Integrate the GEP into key performance indicators (2026-2028)

**Observation:** without a change in political incentives, local officials will continue to optimize the variable they know (GDP growth, land valuation), which will reproduce Stasis.

**Decision:** integrate the Gross Ecosystem Product (GEP) into the KPIs of provincial and county officials, with sufficient weight to change behaviour (indicative threshold: 15 to 20% of the evaluation).

**Owner:** Organization Department of the Central Committee, NDRC.
**Indicator:** share of provinces whose GEP is integrated into the annual balance sheet.

## Recommendation 3 — Open the credit lines of the Two Mountains Bank (2027-2029)

**Observation:** the GEP remains accounting without liquidity as long as no financial vehicle mobilizes it. LGFVs are asphyxiated and cannot carry this function.

**Decision:** create the Two Mountains Bank as a GEP securitization vehicle, with an explicit mandate: finance regeneration, not debt substitution.

**Owner:** People's Bank of China, Ministry of Finance.
**Indicator:** outstanding regeneration-backed securities at 24 months.

## Recommendation 4 — Launch regenerative usufruct certificates on saturated territories (2028-2030)

**Observation:** an unallocated asset cannot be financed. The experiments in Sanming (forest rights) and Lishui (GEP accounting) show that allocating verifiable rights transforms an inactive collective resource into a support for investment.

**Decision:** issue RUCs targeting saturated positions (signature $N_5 \otimes H_5$), with regeneration obligations and verifiable duration.

**Owner:** NDRC, Ministry of Natural Resources, pilot provinces.
**Indicator:** area covered by RUCs at 36 months.

## Recommendation 5 — Anticipate resistances and transition costs (cross-cutting, 2026-2032)

**Observation:** the TRR has losers (provincial bureaucracy, sectors backed by land rent, declining territories) and costs (jobs destroyed before being recreated, fiscal costs, political costs). The report does not conceal them (Chapter 16), but they must be anticipated.

**Decision:** set up a mechanism for monitoring resistances and costs, with support mechanisms (retraining, territorial compensation, differentiated timetable).

**Owner:** State Council, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security.
**Indicator:** coverage rate of support schemes.

# FOREWORD — GENESIS AND EPISTEMOLOGY OF THE MODEL

Conventional macroeconomics (DSGE models) treats the economy as a continuous space of variables adjusting mechanically towards equilibrium. This approach structurally fails to anticipate abrupt ruptures (1929, 2008, Chinese stasis) because it ignores the discrete, informational and threshold-based nature of complex systems. The topological model $\mathrm{Cl}(6,6)$ proposes an alternative framework.

## The native framework: traditional Chinese categories

Traditional Chinese categories — *Yi Jing* (64 configurations), *Wuxing* (5 phases), *Bagua* (8 trigrams) — have described the structure of discrete complex systems for more than two millennia. The $\mathrm{Cl}(6,6)$ formalism joins this description: it provides its rigorous mathematical expression. What the Chinese tradition had discerned empirically, the contemporary formalism confirms structurally.

The 64 hexagrams of the *Yi Jing* are the 64 configurations of the space $\{0,1\}^6$. The 5 phases of the *Wuxing* are the 5 stable states that survive the nilpotency filter. The 8 trigrams of the *Bagua* are the 8 structural configurations of Group A. These are the same objects, described in two different languages.

## Why this language for this audience

The recourse to traditional Chinese categories is not a cultural ornament, nor a concession to fashion. It is an assumed argumentative choice, for three reasons.

**First reason: internal legitimacy.** Chinese political legitimacy has historically been founded on the sovereign's capacity to maintain harmony between Heaven, Earth and Humans. This harmony is not a ritual formula: it designates a material condition, that of a territory capable of renewing what it produces. An economy that exhausts its land base breaks this condition; an economy that regenerates its territory restores it. The TRR is part of this continuity: by restoring the link between the value produced and the value regenerated, it also restores the *De* (德), the political virtue understood as the effective capacity to hold the order of the three domains (economy, society, ecology). A decision-maker of the Party-State does not need to be told that territorial regeneration is legitimate: it already is in the political tradition that he invokes.

**Second reason: structural convergence.** The $\mathrm{Cl}(6,6)$ formalism and traditional categories describe the same reality — the circulation of energy in a discrete complex system. This is not a metaphor: the 64 hexagrams of the *Yi Jing* are strictly isomorphic to the 64 economic configurations; the 5 phases of the *Wuxing* are the 5 stable states that survive the nilpotency filter; the 12 meridians of traditional Chinese medicine correspond to the 12 sectoral spectral leaves. The Chinese tradition has empirically discerned what the contemporary formalism rigorously formalizes. The report therefore does not ask the reader to adopt a foreign language: it shows him that the language he already knows correctly describes the system he must steer.

**Third reason: economy of communication.** A Chinese decision-maker immediately understands what "taking the pulse of the system" or "unblocking an acupuncture point" means. These expressions are more effective, for this audience, than "measuring spectral observables" or "activating a topological threshold". The report therefore uses them as operational classifiers, not as beliefs. It attributes no predictive power of their own to them: they are coding systems, useful because they are shared.

## Chapter 11 — The TRR as a controlled transition operator

### 11.1. The principle

The Territorial Regeneration Rent consists in constituting a base of new value from the effective regeneration of the territory: soils, forests, water, environments, connectivity. This value becomes the support of a credit and of an investment that do not draw on existing value, but increase it.

The shift operated is the following: **one ceases to monetize a stock of land in exhaustion in order to monetize a flow of regeneration in growth.**

At the level of the configuration, it is a matter of moving from the pair $(S_{\text{eco}}, S_{\text{geo}})$, ephemeral, to a viable pair. The two optima identified by the model are $(F_{\text{eco}}, F_{\text{geo}})$ and $(D_{\text{eco}}, D_{\text{geo}})$, with a viability of around **0.226**, i.e. a factor of about five compared with the current situation.

### 11.2. Why the passage cannot be direct

A direct transition from contraction to expansion — liquidity injections, credit-based stimulus without transformation of the real base — does not respect the structure of the system. It produces a configuration that is coherent in appearance and ephemeral in reality: a bubble.

The authorized trajectory passes through intermediate stages. The model locates them on the diagonals of the regime graph: these are the obligatory tipping points, where the economic configuration and the geographic configuration change together.

The determining threshold is threshold **$N_4$**. As long as it is not crossed, stimulus measures remain absorbed by Stasis. Once crossed under the required conditions, they feed a new cycle.

**The operational principle is simple: one does not finance the exit from Stasis, one builds the conditions that make it possible.**

## Chapter 12 — The experiments already under way

The dispositif is not a theoretical proposal. Its components are already at work in several provinces, each illuminating a different dimension of the passage.

### 12.1. Lishui: the accounting of ecological value

Lishui has developed GEP accounting: the territory is described there not only by its market production, but by the value of the ecological services it provides.

This experiment answers the question of the **base**. It shows that a lightly industrialized territory can present a favourable economic configuration provided that its real wealth is measured and made mobilizable.

### 12.2. Sanming: forest capital and rights

Sanming has restructured the rights over collective forest capital, separating ownership, management and benefit.

This experiment answers the question of the **holder**. An unallocated asset cannot be financed. The allocation of verifiable rights transforms an inactive collective resource into a support for investment.

### 12.3. Ganzhou: compensation and restoration

Ganzhou has implemented ecological compensation schemes associated with environmental restoration programmes.

This experiment answers the question of the **flow**. It establishes that a territory engaged in regeneration can receive a remuneration backed by this regeneration, rather than by a subsidy backed by its backwardness.

### 12.4. Hainan: the institutional framework

Hainan is experimenting with an institutional and land framework adapted to a development model founded on the quality of the environment rather than on the extension of the built environment.

This experiment answers the question of the **geographic constraint**. It shows that lifting enclavement is not only a matter of infrastructure: it also passes through the regime under which the territory is administered.

### 12.5. What these experiments have in common

In all four cases, the same movement is at work: a wealth that is present but unaccounted for becomes an allocated, measurable and financeable asset. The territory ceases to be the passive support of a rent in exhaustion and becomes the producer of a renewed value.

**Message to the decision-maker.** Territorial regeneration does not need external legitimation. It restores a base of legitimacy already present in the Chinese political tradition: the capacity to make things endure.

#### 15.3.1. Two ways of dealing with saturation

Faced with the saturation of an accumulation model, two responses are possible.

The first consists in **displacing the saturation**: territorial expansion, relocation of overcapacity, export of imbalances, financialization of existing assets. This is a response through spatial avoidance. It works as long as there is an available exterior.

The second consists in **transforming the base itself**: regenerate the territory, produce a new value, restore the conditions of an internal cycle. This is a response through regeneration. It does not presuppose an exterior.

The model describes the first as an attempt at direct transition from contraction to expansion. Such a transition does not respect the stages of crossing; it prepares an unfavourable retropolarity, that is to say a return of constraint all the stronger as the initial configuration was coherent in appearance.

### 16.1. Institutional resistances

The TRR is not a neutral reform. It modifies incentives, rents and power relations. Three categories of actors are likely to resist:

- **The provincial bureaucracy**: local officials whose evaluation rests on GDP growth and land valuation lose their main lever of action. The reform of KPIs (integration of the GEP) is the condition of their rallying, but it clashes with their short-term interests.

### 16.4. The trade-offs

The TRR does not escape the classic trade-offs of any structural reform:

- **Speed vs. stability**: the "pure" variant (horizon 2032) is the fastest, but the most demanding in terms of a prepared framework. The "tempered growth" variant (horizon 2035) is slower, but more robust.
- **Centralization vs. local experimentation**: national coordination is necessary for thresholds $S_4$ and $S_5$, but local experimentation is necessary for the RUCs and the pilot zones. The right balance is a national framework with local margins.
- **Rigour vs. compensation**: the reform of KPIs must be rigorous in order to change behaviours, but it must be accompanied by compensation for the losers. Without rigour, it is cosmetic; without compensation, it is politically untenable.

### 16.5. The political conditions for success

The TRR will only succeed if three political conditions are met:

1. **A clear mandate from the top**: the reform of KPIs and the conversion of LGFVs can only be imposed by the State Council, with an explicit mandate and a binding timetable.
2. **A coalition of winners**: the regenerated territories, the regeneration sectors and the beneficiary populations must be mobilized as the political base of the reform.
3. **Management of the losers**: the support mechanisms (retraining, territorial compensation, differentiated timetable) must be scaled to the level of the costs.

**Message to the decision-maker.** The TRR is not a technical reform. It is a political reform, which modifies rents, incentives and power relations. It will only succeed if it is carried by a clear mandate, a coalition of winners and management of the losers. The report provides the technical framework; the political decision remains to be taken.

## Conclusion of Part III

Post-2018 China occupies an **ephemeral** situation: the double Stasis, economic and territorial, with a viability of 0.044. This situation is coherent in its mechanisms and unstable in its duration.

There exist **11 viable situations out of 400**. Reaching one of them presupposes crossing a threshold, in a determined order, with a complete dispositif: measure the regenerated value (GEP), allocate it (RUCs), make it financeable (Two Mountains Bank).

The pilot provinces show its components. The administrative architecture provides its steering at three scales. The timetable sets the deadline: $\mathcal{I}_{\text{Stasis}} < 0.30$ in 2032.

The restoration of the Mandate of Heaven and the diplomacy of regeneration are the two extensions of this passage: one towards internal legitimacy, the other towards an external standard. In both cases, the content is the same — a territory that renews what it engages.

The TRR has resistances and costs. It is not a technical reform, but a political reform, which will only succeed if it is carried by a clear mandate, a coalition of winners and management of the losers.

**The central message for the decision-maker:** the TRR is not one sectoral policy among others. It is a **change of geo-economic situation**, which moves the system from an ephemeral situation (viability 0.044) to a viable situation (viability ≈ 0.226), i.e. a factor of ~5. The window is dated. The means engaged between 2026 and 2032 determine less the growth rate of the next year than the configuration in which the country will enter at the end of the Plan.

Any passage from a contractive cycle to an expansive cycle must pass through a **transition airlock**. A forced passage produces an unfavourable tipping. This is the common lesson of the nine historical sequences recorded.
